Provider configuration

Email SMTP and Twilio credentials are configured by the project administrator in the local .env file. Your preferences do not turn on an unconfigured provider.

  1. Set the SMTP or Twilio credentials in .env.
  2. Set ENABLE_EMAIL=true or ENABLE_SMS=true.
  3. Restart Flask. Never share or commit your credentials.
SMS delivery may incur charges and trial accounts may restrict recipient numbers. For account security, essential password reset emails are sent independently of notification preferences.
